## Run the comparison

1. Complete hiring-intake.md with the hiring manager before writing the job ad.
2. Prepare five to seven job-related criteria and synthetic sample evidence. Use screening-calibration.md and screening-calibration.csv to compare independent reviewer interpretations.
3. Confirm the criteria version, owner and reopening conditions before reviewing live applications.
4. Use vendor-evaluation.md and vendor-comparison.csv for observed vendor behaviour; write notes before scores.

Templates and column explanations are available in four languages. CSV column keys remain English so files can be compared and imported consistently. Use worksheet-guide.md for the column meanings.

## Limits and decision boundaries

These documents support human review. Agreement does not make a criterion fair, lawful or accurate. Vendor observations are not certification or proof of production quality. Do not publish filled-in worksheets containing candidate or internal business data. The agenda and worksheets are working documents, not claims about application features.
