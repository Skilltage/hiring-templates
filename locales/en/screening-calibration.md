# Screening calibration

## Meeting agenda

- 0–5 minutes: confirm the role outcome and the decisions this screen can support.
- 5–12 minutes: examine five to seven prepared criteria; challenge hard requirements and separate preferences.
- 12–22 minutes: reviewers independently assess the same synthetic evidence.
- 22–27 minutes: compare disagreements and revise ambiguous wording or evidence expectations.
- 27–30 minutes: confirm the owner, version, unresolved questions and reopening conditions.

## Confirm before use

- [ ] Every criterion is tied to the work and has an explicit priority.
- [ ] Expected evidence and the treatment of missing information are explicit.
- [ ] Reviewers assessed the same samples independently.
- [ ] Material disagreements produced a documented wording or evidence decision.
- [ ] Unresolved questions have an owner and a review point.
- [ ] Reopening requires new role information, a material error or a documented review—not a preferred candidate.

## Limits and decision boundaries

These documents support human review. Agreement does not make a criterion fair, lawful or accurate. Vendor observations are not certification or proof of production quality. Do not publish filled-in worksheets containing candidate or internal business data. The agenda and worksheets are working documents, not claims about application features.
